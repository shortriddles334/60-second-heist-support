Privacy Policy

Nimbus Bikes LLC · Last updated November 5, 2026

Game support

Game data

You can play without a Nimbus Bikes account. We do not show ads or run gameplay analytics in the game.

Your settings, vault progress, times, and medals stay in app storage on your device or in your browser. We do not receive these saves or sync them between devices. Your device or browser may back up local data according to your settings.

Purchases

Google Play handles Android purchases, and Apple handles iPhone and iPad purchases through StoreKit. The game reads the product and purchase status to unlock the full game. On Android, it uses a purchase token to acknowledge the purchase with Google Play and saves an ownership marker on the device for offline play. The game does not ask for or receive your payment card details.

On Android, the Google Play Billing library sends billing diagnostics to Google when the app uses the billing service, including when it checks for ownership. These can include operation results, errors, timing, app and Android version, device model and build, language and region settings, time zone, carrier, and network type. Google receives this information to operate and maintain Play Billing. See Google's privacy policy.

On iPhone and iPad, the game uses verified StoreKit transactions to confirm access and finish purchases. See Apple's App Store privacy information for how Apple processes App Store account, transaction, and device information.

This support site

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. We do not add analytics scripts or tracking cookies to these pages. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Clear local progress

Use Reset Progress in Settings to remove your game progress. You can clear app storage through your device settings where available, or clear the site's storage in your browser, to remove local settings and saves. These actions do not change purchase records held by the store.

Contact

If you email us for support, Nimbus Bikes LLC receives your email address and the details you choose to send. We use this information to respond and keep it as needed to handle your request.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence, email nimbusbikespdx@gmail.com.
